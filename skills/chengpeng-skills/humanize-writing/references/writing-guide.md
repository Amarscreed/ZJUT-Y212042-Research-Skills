# Academic Writing Quality Guide

## Readability Targets

| Metric | Good | Acceptable | Poor |
|--------|------|-----------|------|
| Flesch Reading Ease | 25-45 | 15-25 or 45-55 | <15 or >55 |
| Flesch-Kincaid Grade | 12-16 | 10-12 or 16-18 | <10 or >18 |
| Avg Sentence Length | 18-25 words | 15-18 or 25-30 | <15 or >30 |
| Passive Voice | <20% | 20-30% | >30% |
| Hedge Word Density | <2 per 100 words | 2-5 per 100 words | >5 per 100 words |

## Common AI-ism Patterns (with Replacement)

| AI-ism | Natural Replacement |
|--------|-------------------|
| "In this paper, we propose..." | "We propose..." |
| "Experimental results demonstrate that..." | "Experiments show..." |
| "It is worth noting that..." | (Delete) |
| "The model achieves state-of-the-art performance" | "The model achieves a CheXbert F1 of 0.73, surpassing prior work by 4.2%" |
| "A comprehensive analysis reveals..." | "Analysis shows..." |
| "This work makes the following contributions: (1)..." | "This work makes three contributions: ..." |
| "The rest of this paper is organized as follows" | (Delete - readers know) |

## Sentence-Level Checklist

- [ ] Every claim has supporting evidence (citation, number, or reasoning)
- [ ] No sentence starts with "However" twice in a paragraph
- [ ] Active voice preferred where clear
- [ ] No dangling modifiers
- [ ] No run-on sentences (>40 words)
- [ ] Technical terms are defined on first use
- [ ] Pronoun references are unambiguous
